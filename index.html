About Us

Get to Know Us Better

2020 Founded

InAmigos Foundation was founded on September 23, 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla. It is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization, licensed by the Central Government, with its base in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh.

The foundation holds 80G & 12A certifications, ensuring transparency and tax-exempt benefits for donors. It is also CSR-1 registered for corporate partnerships, NITI Aayog registered to align with national development goals, and holds the IAF ISO 9001:2015 certification for quality standards in operations and service delivery.

With a network of dedicated volunteers and corporate partners, InAmigos works across 28 states in India, bringing positive change through food distribution, education, women empowerment, animal welfare, and environmental sustainability.

Our Impact

How Change Happens

Every initiative follows a simple path — identifying a challenge, taking action, and creating lasting impact in communities across India.

01
Challenge

Communities face barriers — lack of food, education, opportunities, or care for vulnerable people and animals.

02
Action

Volunteers and partners mobilize through targeted projects — distributing essentials, teaching skills, planting trees, and rescuing animals.

03
Impact

Improved access to learning, greater inclusion, environmental awareness, personal development, and compassion for all.
